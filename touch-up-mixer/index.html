Built for real repair work

A practical colour-mixing helper for the bench.

Touch-Up Mixer is designed for small repairs where judgement still matters. It gives you a repeatable way to sample, compare, test, adjust, and record what worked, without pretending a photo can make the final call for you.

What it helps with

From first sample to saved repair record.

Photo-guided sampling

Place match, repair, and ignore circles so the colour options are driven by the surrounding finish, not glare, dirt, hardware, or damaged material.

Your own palette

Build palettes from the colours and media you actually use, including acrylics, stains, dyes, shellac tints, fillers, putty, wax, and pigments.

Practical mix suggestions

Get starting recipes that respect the selected medium, with shorter suggestions for translucent and solid repair materials where restraint matters.

Repair records

Save jobs with photos, sample points, palette choice, suggested mixes, notes, rating, and after photos for follow-up work or future reference.

Palette first

Mix from what is on your bench.

A small honest palette is more useful than a large imaginary one. Touch-Up Mixer changes its suggestions based on the medium, opacity, clear modifiers, and colour set you choose.

Keep the useful details

Save jobs, continue later, and share the result.

Saved jobs can restore the target photo, sample point, palette, and context back into the main workflow. Add an after photo when the repair is finished, then export a simple before/after image or printable PDF.

Local by design

Photos stay on your device.

Touch-Up Mixer does not upload photos, create accounts, track users, or collect analytics. Saved jobs, palettes, and notes are stored locally on your device.
