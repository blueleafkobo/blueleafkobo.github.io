For the joy of making.

A keepsake for a clever print. A page for your design journal. A pen drawing of something you brought to life. BlueprintPlotter celebrates the design, without asking you to prepare a production drawing.

See every side

Arrange front, top, right and isometric views, or try a custom angle. Resize the panels to give your favourite view more room.

Make it yours

Choose grids, borders and colours. Add a title, date and design details, with a bottom band or compact corner title layout.

Put pen to paper

Export SVG drawing layers, select pen colours in your plotting software, and use optional fill strokes for larger lettering.

From model to drawing in three steps

  1. Bring your design. Import an ASCII or binary STL file.
  2. Compose your sheet. Choose views and styling, then review calculated dimensions and hidden edges.
  3. Export your artwork. Choose Preview Style for the visual sheet, or Cricut Pen Layers for a transparent plotting SVG.

A few things to know

BlueprintPlotter is a Mac app in preparation for release. Pricing, download availability and final system requirements will be published here at launch.

It imports STL models; it is not a 3D modelling editor. Dimensions and hidden edges should be reviewed before export. The output is intended for creative artwork and visual documentation, not certified engineering or manufacturing plans.

For Cricut workflows, you assign the Pen operation and use Attach in Design Space. BlueprintPlotter does not control the machine directly. Hardware, pens and external software are not included.

BlueprintPlotter is an independent Blue Leaf Kobo app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cricut.
