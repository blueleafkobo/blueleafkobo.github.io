Few taps, clear result

Built around the way the work actually happens.

  1. Create the job. Add a label, with optional client, address, and notes.
  2. Capture the before photos. Take as many as the work needs.
  3. Return for the after photos. Line each one up with the camera guide.
  4. Share or archive. Send the result and keep the record on your iPhone.

Local by design

Your job photos stay on your iPhone.

MadeGood has no account, tracking, analytics, advertising, or cloud sync. Photos leave the app only when you choose to share them.
