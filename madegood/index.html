Common questions

MadeGood FAQ

Does MadeGood require an account?

No. MadeGood does not require an account. Jobs stay local unless you share them or add them to an optional team folder you choose in Files.

Is MadeGood free?

MadeGood is free to start. You can use the full job-photo workflow and complete 10 exports before upgrading.

What does MadeGood Pro unlock?

MadeGood Pro unlocks unlimited sharing and export. It is a one-time App Store purchase, not a subscription.

Can I organise a larger job?

Yes. Optional Job Areas can group photo pairs by room, section, stage, or task while simpler jobs can remain one list.

How can I share the finished work?

Send combined before-and-after images or separate originals for one pair, an area, or the whole job through Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files, or another app on your iPhone.

Does MadeGood store my photos online?

MadeGood stores jobs locally by default. Optional Team Sharing writes selected jobs to a folder you choose in Files; MadeGood does not run a sync server, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

You choose where work goes

Local first, with optional team folders.

MadeGood has no account, tracking, analytics, advertising, or developer-operated sync server. Photos leave local storage only when you share them, use the Touch-Up Mixer handoff, or add a job to a team folder you selected.
