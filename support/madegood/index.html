Camera, Photos, and Location

MadeGood uses Camera access to capture job photos and Photo Library access to import existing images. Location access is optional and only helps fill a job address.

If access is not working, open iOS Settings, find MadeGood, and check the relevant permission.

Matching After Photos

When you take an after photo, MadeGood places the original before image over the camera preview as a guide. Match the phone orientation, position, and distance as closely as practical.

Job Areas

Job Areas are optional groups for rooms, furniture sections, work stages, or other parts of a larger job. Jobs that do not need areas can continue as a simple list of photo pairs.

Free Exports and MadeGood Pro

MadeGood is free to start with 10 completed exports. After those exports are used, MadeGood Pro unlocks unlimited sharing and export.

An export is counted when a share/export action is completed. Creating jobs, adding photos, organising Job Areas, and viewing existing work do not require Pro.

Restore Purchase

If you already purchased MadeGood Pro, open Settings in MadeGood and choose Restore Purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.

MadeGood Pro is a one-time unlock for unlimited sharing and export, not a subscription.

Export or Share Troubleshooting

If sharing does not complete, check that the selected destination app is installed and available, then try a smaller selection such as one photo pair or one Job Area.

You can send results through Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files, or the apps you already use on iPhone.

Storage and Backups

Jobs and photos are stored locally on your iPhone. MadeGood does not create an account or sync your jobs to Blue Leaf Kobo.

If you use Team Sharing, choose the same existing shared folder on each person's device. Use Refresh Jobs to find jobs and Sync Job to exchange changes. The storage provider controls access to the folder.

Deleting the app may also delete its locally stored jobs and photos. Keep important shared images or other records outside the app.
