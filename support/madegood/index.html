MadeGood Support

Help with before and after job photos, camera matching, Job Areas, sharing, and local storage.

MadeGood Privacy Policy

Contact

Email blueleafkobo+support@gmail.com for MadeGood support.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Camera, Photos, and Location

MadeGood uses Camera access to capture job photos and Photo Library access to import existing images. Location access is optional and only helps fill a job address.

If access is not working, open iOS Settings, find MadeGood, and check the relevant permission.

Matching After Photos

When you take an after photo, MadeGood places the original before image over the camera preview as a guide. Match the phone orientation, position, and distance as closely as practical.

Job Areas

Job Areas are optional groups for rooms, furniture sections, work stages, or other parts of a larger job. Jobs that do not need areas can continue as a simple list of photo pairs.

Storage and Backups

Jobs and photos are stored locally on your iPhone. MadeGood does not create an account or sync your jobs to Blue Leaf Kobo.

Deleting the app may also delete its locally stored jobs and photos. Keep important shared images or other records outside the app.
