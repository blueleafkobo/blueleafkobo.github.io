Help with sampling repair colours, setting up palettes, checking mixes, and managing saved Repairs or optional Jobs.

Touch-Up Mixer Privacy Policy

Contact

Email blueleafkobo+support@gmail.com for support with Touch-Up Mixer.

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened.

Photo Access

Touch-Up Mixer uses photos only for colour sampling, mix checking, saved Repair and Job records, and before/after sharing.

If photo or camera access is not working, check iOS Settings for Touch-Up Mixer and confirm that Camera and Photos permissions are enabled.

Saved Repairs, Jobs, and Palettes

Repairs can remain standalone or be grouped into optional Jobs. Repairs, Jobs, palettes, addresses, photos, and notes are stored locally on your device. Touch-Up Mixer does not create an account or sync this information to Blue Leaf Kobo.

Deleting the app may also delete locally stored Touch-Up Mixer data.

Job Address Autofill

Location access is optional and requested only when you choose to fill a Job address from your current location. Touch-Up Mixer stores the resulting address only if you keep it; it does not save your coordinates.

Mix Suggestions

Suggested mixes are practical starting points. Real repairs still depend on the material, lighting, application thickness, drying time, sheen, texture, and your eye.

If the app says the palette has limited close matches, try a more suitable palette or approach the repair in layers with additional adjustment.
