MadeGood Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MadeGood handles job details, photos, optional location access, in-app purchase status, sharing/export, and local storage.

Blue Leaf Kobo is the support and website name used for MadeGood.

MadeGood Support

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Overview

MadeGood is a before-and-after job photo tool for iPhone. The app does not require an account and does not track users.

Jobs and Photos

MadeGood stores job labels, optional client details, addresses, notes, and job photos locally on your device. This information is not sent to Blue Leaf Kobo or another service by the app.

MadeGood may ask for Camera or Photo Library access so you can take or choose photos. Photos are processed on your device to create before-and-after images.

Location

Location access is optional. If you choose Use Current Location while creating or editing a job, MadeGood asks iOS to turn your current location into an address. MadeGood does not continuously track your location or send it to Blue Leaf Kobo.

In-App Purchases

MadeGood Pro is a one-time App Store unlock. Purchases and restore-purchase checks are handled by Apple through the App Store. MadeGood uses the purchase result to unlock unlimited sharing/export, but Blue Leaf Kobo does not receive your payment details.

Sharing and Exports

When you choose to share or export a job, area, or photo pair, the generated files may contain photos, labels, notes, client details, addresses, or other information you entered. The files leave MadeGood only when you choose a destination such as Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files, or another app on your iPhone. The selected destination handles the shared content under its own privacy policy.

Analytics, Tracking, and Accounts

MadeGood does not create user accounts, include advertising, collect analytics or crash reports, or use information for tracking or profiling.

Retention and Deletion

Jobs remain on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Deleting a job removes its locally saved job information and media. Deleting MadeGood may also delete all locally stored MadeGood data.

Children's Privacy

MadeGood is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email blueleafkobo+support@gmail.com.
