Touch-Up Mixer Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Touch-Up Mixer handles photos, saved repair information, palettes, and app settings.

Home Support

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Overview

Touch-Up Mixer is designed to help users sample colours from photos, compare repair colours, and create touch-up mix suggestions. The app does not require an account and does not track users.

Photos and Colour Sampling

Touch-Up Mixer may ask for access to the camera or photo library so you can take or choose photos of repair areas, colour samples, and palette swatches.

Photos are processed on your device for colour sampling. Touch-Up Mixer does not upload repair photos, paint sample photos, palette photos, or saved repair photos to Blue Leaf Kobo.

Saved Repairs and Palettes

Saved repairs, palettes, colour samples, and related app settings are stored locally on your device. Blue Leaf Kobo does not receive this information.

If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be deleted by iOS.

Analytics, Tracking, and Accounts

Touch-Up Mixer does not create user accounts, does not include third-party advertising, and does not use tracking for advertising or profiling.

Touch-Up Mixer does not collect analytics or crash reports for Blue Leaf Kobo.

Children's Privacy

Touch-Up Mixer is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
